Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

At Elevate Webworks, accessible from https://elevate-webworks.vercel.app/, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Elevate Webworks and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at elevateweb.india@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you express interest in obtaining information about us or our services, when you contact us directly, or when you fill out forms on our website.

  • Personal Data: Name, email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, business name, and any other project requirements or communications you provide when submitting inquiries via our Google Forms or direct mail links.
  • Usage Data: We automatically collect certain information when you visit, use, or navigate the site. This includes IP addresses, browser specifications, operating system, referring URLs, device info, and details about how and when you interact with our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect or receive in various ways to support our operations, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website services.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our website performance and features.
  • Understand and analyze how visitors interact with our website (using analytics tools).
  • Communicate with you, either directly or through one of our partners, to respond to support tickets, inquiries, or update you on project deliverables.
  • Send you emails, invoices, and service updates related to active projects.
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues or potential fraudulent activities.

3. Log Files & Analytics

Elevate Webworks follows a standard procedure of using log files and third-party tracking services. We use Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) to collect information about how our visitors interact with the site. The information tracked includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and version
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamps of your visit
  • Referring and exit pages
  • The number of clicks and pages viewed

These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of this information is to analyze trends, administer the site, track users' movement on the website, and gather demographic information.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Elevate Webworks uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. To know more detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers, it can be found at the browsers' respective websites.

5. Third-Party Services

Our site contains links to other external web services, including Google Forms (for service intake/checkout), WhatsApp (for communication), and social platforms. This Privacy Policy applies solely to information collected by Elevate Webworks. We encourage you to read the privacy statements of any third-party websites or services you visit, as we cannot take responsibility for their privacy practices.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect the security of any personal data we process. However, please remember that no transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights (DPDPA & Global Laws)

Depending on your location (especially under the India Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or global regulations like the GDPR), you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • The right to request the deletion or erasure of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to withdraw your consent to data processing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us directly at elevateweb.india@gmail.com. We will respond to your request within the timeline required by applicable law.

8. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
